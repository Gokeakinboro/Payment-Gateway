Data Deletion Request

PayPrompt by Paylode Services Limited  ·  CBN-Licensed PSSP (No. CBN/PAY/2024/001847)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and Google Play's data deletion policy, you have the right to request erasure of your personal data collected through the PayPrompt app. This page explains what data we hold, what can be deleted, and how to submit a request.

Data we collect via PayPrompt

CategoryExamplesCan be deleted?
Profile dataName, email, phone numberYes — on account deletion

Wallet / membership dataClosed Wallet balance, membership recordsYes — on account deletion
KYC / identity dataBVN, NIN, identity documentsRetained for 5 years (regulatory)

Settlement recordsPayout details, bank account referencesRetained for 5 years (regulatory)
Note: Financial and KYC records must be retained for a minimum of 5 years as required by the Central Bank of Nigeria's AML regulations and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. These records are not visible in any user-facing interface after account deletion.

How to submit a data deletion request

Email our Data Protection Officer from the email address associated with your PayPrompt account:

support@paylodeservices.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request — PayPrompt
Please include:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your registered phone number
  • The specific data you want deleted (or state "all data" for a full erasure request)
Email a data deletion request

What happens next

  • We will acknowledge your request within 72 hours.
  • We may ask you to verify your identity before processing.
  • Eligible data will be deleted within 30 days.
  • You will receive a written confirmation once the deletion is complete.
  • Regulatory records are retained separately and are never surfaced to users.

Delete your account (and all associated data)

The fastest way to request full data deletion is to delete your PayPrompt account. Visit our Account Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

Questions or complaints

Contact our Data Protection Officer at support@paylodeservices.com. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.
