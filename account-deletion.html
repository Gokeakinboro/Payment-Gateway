Delete Your Account

Payprompt by Paylode Services Limited  ·  CBN-Licensed PSSP (No. CBN/PAY/2024/001847)

You have the right to request deletion of your Payprompt account and the personal data associated with it. Please read the information below carefully before submitting your request.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data — including transaction history, wallet balances, and saved settings — will be removed as described below.

What gets deleted

  • Your Payprompt login credentials (email and password)
  • Your profile information (name, phone number, business details)
  • Your KYC/verification data, subject to regulatory retention requirements
  • Your wallet and membership records within Closed Wallet
  • Your notification preferences and device tokens

What we are required to retain

As a CBN-licensed Payment Solution Service Provider, we are required by law to retain certain financial and transaction records for a minimum of 5 years after account deletion, in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, CBN Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations, and related financial reporting obligations. Records retained for this purpose include:

  • Transaction references and amounts
  • KYC/identity verification records
  • Settlement and payout records

How to request account deletion

Option 1 — Via the Payprompt app

  1. Open the Payprompt app and sign in
  2. Tap Profile (bottom right of the screen)
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the action — your account will be scheduled for deletion within 30 days

Option 2 — Via email

Send an email to our support team from the email address registered to your Payprompt account:

support@paylodeservices.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request — Payprompt
Include your registered email address and phone number so we can verify your identity. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

Email us to delete your account

Timeline

  • Requests are processed within 30 days of submission.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
  • Regulatory records (see above) are retained separately and will not appear in any user-facing interface.

Questions

For any questions about this process, contact us at support@paylodeservices.com or visit our Privacy Policy.
